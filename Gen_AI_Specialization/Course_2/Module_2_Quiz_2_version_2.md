## Graded Quiz: Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques & Applications

---

### Question 1

A user wants help selecting a car using an AI system. What is the advantage of using an interview pattern prompt for this task?

* **A)** The AI gives the cheapest car without explanation.
* **B)** The AI provides standard answers based on car popularity.
* **C)** The AI filters out most questions for simplicity.
* **D)** The AI gathers preferences through dialogue to suggest options. **(Correct Answer)**

> **Explainer:** Interactive questioning via the interview pattern enables dynamic dialogue to gather user preferences and deliver tailored recommendations.

---

### Question 2

Jennifer wants to request some useful information about a complex medical condition using a large language model. Which among the following techniques should she employ on the text prompt to ensure that the generated content is relevant, accurate, and precise for this specialized field?

* **A)** Bias mitigation
* **B)** Domain expertise **(Correct Answer)**
* **C)** Task specification
* **D)** Framing

> **Explainer:** Domain expertise is essential to improve the reliability and accuracy of LLMs when generating specialized professional content.

---

### Question 3

You’re designing prompts to avoid stereotypes in AI responses about different cultures. What should you focus on?

* **A)** Do you understand?
* **B)** Zero-shot prompting
* **C)** Domain expertise
* **D)** Bias mitigation **(Correct Answer)**

> **Explainer:** Applying bias mitigation techniques helps ensure fairness, neutrality, and cultural sensitivity in AI-generated outputs.

---

### Question 4

An AI is being trained to handle algebra problems. Which prompt best supports Chain-of-Thought reasoning?

* **A)** Provide a list of similar problems without answers
* **B)** Use humor to encourage problem-solving
* **C)** Include solved examples with explanation **(Correct Answer)**
* **D)** Include the answer only with no context

> **Explainer:** Including solved examples with explanations guides the model through structured reasoning and multi-step logic progression.

---

### Question 5

An AI is advising patients on potential treatment options based on symptoms and test results. What is the best prompting technique?

* **A)** Show options ranked by popularity
* **B)** Ask the patient to pick one option up front
* **C)** Prompt the model to reason through the pros and cons of each treatment **(Correct Answer)**
* **D)** Present a list of outcomes and let them guess

> **Explainer:** This follows Tree-of-Thought reasoning, allowing the system to explore and evaluate multiple treatment pathways systematically.

---

### Question 6

Your prompt asks the AI to brainstorm several taglines for a campaign, then analyze their strengths, and pick the best. What is this approach called?

* **A)** Do you understand?
* **B)** Random option selection and framing
* **C)** Playoff method involving generation and self-evaluation **(Correct Answer)**
* **D)** Few-shot prompting

> **Explainer:** The playoff method combines creativity and judgment by having the model generate diverse alternatives, compare them, and self-select the winner.

---

### Question 7

You want an AI to critique a digital poster design and suggest visual enhancements. Which prompt strategy should be used?

* **A)** Multimodal prompt that feeds in the image and asks a specific design question **(Correct Answer)**
* **B)** Use bias mitigation to guide the response
* **C)** Describe the image instead of uploading it
* **D)** Send an image URL with no additional instructions

> **Explainer:** Multimodal prompting enables visual-text reasoning by combining visual assets with targeted textual instructions simultaneously.
