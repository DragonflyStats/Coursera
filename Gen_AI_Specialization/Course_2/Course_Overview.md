## Course Summary: Course 2 - Generative AI: Prompt Engineering Basics

### Overview

This module introduces the core concepts of prompt engineering in generative AI, equipping you with essential techniques to design effective prompts, leverage best practices, and assess common prompt engineering tools to achieve desired, high-quality responses.

### Key Learning Objectives

* **Define a prompt and its key elements**: Understand how structural building blocks—such as clear instructions and background context—shape AI outputs.
* **Explain the concept and relevance of prompt engineering**: Recognize why crafting precise instructions is crucial for guiding generative models toward accurate, relevant, and logical results.
* **Apply best practices**: Utilize techniques like the persona pattern, structured examples, and iterative testing to control tone, style, and intent.
* **Explore common tools**: Discover tools designed to suggest, structure, and refine prompts across diverse use cases (such as creative marketing or genre-specific writing).
