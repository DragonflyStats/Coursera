Here is the revised list of key technical terms with a descriptive paragraph for each:

* **Interview Pattern**
This design pattern involves structuring a prompt to engage the AI in an interactive, step-by-step dialogue rather than requesting a single static answer. By asking questions successively and gathering user preferences dynamically, the system can deliver highly tailored and accurate recommendations.
* **Domain Expertise**
This technique involves embedding specialized professional terminology, legal references, or deep contextual knowledge directly into a prompt. Doing so guides large language models to produce more rigorous, accurate, and relevant outputs tailored to specialized fields like medicine, law, or advanced engineering.
* **Bias Mitigation**
This practice applies explicit instructions and constraints to prompts to ensure that AI-generated responses remain neutral, fair, and free of stereotypes. It is crucial for maintaining cultural sensitivity and impartiality across diverse user groups and sensitive applications.
* **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning**
This prompt engineering framework guides the model to "think aloud" by breaking complex problems down into sequential, intermediate logic steps or worked examples. It significantly improves problem-solving performance on logic-heavy tasks like mathematics and multi-step programming.
* **Tree-of-Thought (ToT) Reasoning**
An extension of Chain-of-Thought reasoning, this approach allows the AI model to explore, evaluate, and compare multiple reasoning branches or pathways in parallel. It is especially useful for complex decision-making scenarios where the system must systematically weigh the pros and cons of different options.
* **Playoff Method (Generation and Self-Evaluation)**
This method combines AI creativity with critical judgment by having the model first generate multiple diverse alternatives (such as headlines or ideas), critique their respective strengths against specific criteria, and finally self-select the winning solution.
* **Multimodal Prompting**
This technique combines visual assets (such as uploaded images, diagrams, or charts) with targeted textual instructions simultaneously. It enables the model to perform cross-modal reasoning, analyzing visual and text-based data together to provide contextual insights.
* **Few-Shot Prompting**
This approach provides the model with a few concrete examples of input-output pairs within the prompt. It helps the AI better interpret the intended style, format, structure, and level of detail required for the task.
* **Zero-Shot Prompting**
This technique involves asking the model to perform a task without providing any prior examples or demonstrations. It relies entirely on the model's pre-trained knowledge base to understand and execute the instructions correctly.
* **Contextual Guidance**
This foundational practice supplies essential background information, framing, and situational circumstances within a prompt. It sets the proper scene so that the model can align its generated response with the user's explicit goals and operating environment.
