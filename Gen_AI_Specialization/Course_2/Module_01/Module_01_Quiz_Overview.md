## Prompt Engineering for Generative AI Quiz

This assessment evaluates fundamental concepts, core techniques, and practical applications in **prompt engineering for generative AI**. The content covers the following key thematic areas:

* **Anatomy of a Prompt & Core Building Blocks:** Understanding the structural elements of a well-crafted prompt—specifically **instructions**, **context**, and the role they play in framing requests to align outputs with user goals.
* **Best Practices & Advanced Patterns:** Applying effective strategies such as the **persona pattern** to guide tone, voice, and perspective, as well as utilizing structured **few-shot examples** to improve model intent interpretation and response accuracy.
* **Genre-Specific & Specialized Applications:** Tailoring prompt structures for specific domains (such as creative writing, sci-fi character arcs, or corporate product copy) to achieve targeted, high-quality results.
* **Iterative Testing & Refinement:** Recognizing the importance of systematic testing to identify missing gaps, evaluate alignment against goals, and refine prompts through multiple iterations.
* **Tool Functionality & Expert Strategies:** Exploring how prompt engineering tools assist in drafting, structuring, and optimizing prompts, alongside expert recommendations such as asking the model directly for guidance when uncertain about how to prompt for a task.
