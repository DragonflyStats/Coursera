## Practice Quiz: AI Architecture Understanding

---

### Question 1

A technology company is developing a new chatbot that can process entire paragraphs and understand context across many sentences. Which AI architecture is best known for using "self-attention" to achieve this capability?

* **A)** Linear Regression
* **B)** Large Language Models (LLMs) built with the Transformer architecture. **(Correct Answer)**
* **C)** Random Forest
* **D)** Logistic Regression

> **Explainer:** Transformer-based Large Language Models leverage self-attention mechanisms to weigh relationships between words and process long contexts across entire paragraphs simultaneously.

### Question 2

A university student wants to use an AI tool that can easily switch between different languages for translation tasks and can handle long pieces of text. Which technology made this possible?

* **A)** Linear Regression
* **B)** Large Language Models (LLMs) built with the Transformer architecture. **(Correct Answer)**
* **C)** Random Forest
* **D)** Logistic Regression

> **Explainer:** Transformers power modern multilingual translation models and LLMs, enabling them to process lengthy sequences and accurately handle context across different languages.

### Question 3

A software team is researching the history of AI architectures. They discover that one key breakthrough allowed neural networks to move beyond simple single-layer models and begin solving complex, multi-layered problems. Which statement best explains why the backpropagation algorithm was a pivotal advancement in AI architecture?

* **A)** It replaced neural networks entirely with rule-based expert systems that were easier to program.
* **B)** It created decision trees that could split data using rules without requiring any training data.
* **C)** It introduced self-attention mechanisms that helped networks understand relationships between words in long sentences.
* **D)** It allowed multi-layer neural networks to learn from errors and adjust their connections, enabling them to recognize complex patterns like handwriting and speech. **(Correct Answer)**

> **Explainer:** Backpropagation computes gradients of error with respect to network weights, allowing multi-layer neural networks to learn and adapt across hidden layers to recognize complex patterns.

### Question 4

A professor is teaching about early AI architectures and explains that the very first neural networks had a significant limitation — they could only solve problems where data was clearly separable and could not handle more complex, layered patterns. Which statement best explains why this limitation was a critical turning point in the evolution of AI architecture?

* **A)** It proved that neural networks were not useful, leading researchers to permanently abandon them in favor of traditional programming.
* **B)** It demonstrated that AI systems only needed more data and faster hardware to solve any problem, without changing the architecture.
* **C)** It confirmed that rule-based expert systems were superior to neural networks in every situation, making them the dominant AI approach forever.
* **D)** It showed that single-layer networks like the Perceptron had boundaries in what they could learn, which directly motivated the development of multi-layer networks capable of solving more complex problems. **(Correct Answer)**

> **Explainer:** Recognizing the limitations of single-layer perceptrons (such as their inability to solve non-linear problems like XOR) drove researchers to develop multi-layer architectures and training algorithms.

### Question 5

An AI engineer is reviewing key milestones in AI history before giving a presentation. One breakthrough involved a shift from rule-based systems to learning-based AI models. Which advance best describes this change in architecture?

* **A)** The invention of computer hardware.
* **B)** The introduction of rule-based expert systems.
* **C)** The development of sorting algorithms.
* **D)** The rise of artificial neural networks using backpropagation. **(Correct Answer)**

> **Explainer:** The rise of artificial neural networks trained via algorithms like backpropagation marked the historical pivot from rigid, human-coded rule-based systems to flexible, data-driven learning models.
