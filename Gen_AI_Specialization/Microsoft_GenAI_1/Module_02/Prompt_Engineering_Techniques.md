Here is a concise summary of the key technical terms and concepts covered in prompt engineering:

* **Interview Pattern**
Structuring a prompt to engage the AI in an interactive, step-by-step dialogue rather than requesting a single static answer. By asking questions successively and gathering preferences dynamically, the system delivers highly tailored recommendations.
* **Domain Expertise**
Embedding specialized professional terminology, legal references, or deep contextual knowledge directly into a prompt. Doing so guides large language models to produce more rigorous, accurate, and relevant outputs tailored to specialized fields.
* **Bias Mitigation**
Applying explicit instructions and constraints to prompts to ensure that AI-generated responses remain neutral, fair, and free of stereotypes. It is crucial for maintaining cultural sensitivity and impartiality across diverse user groups.
* **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning**
A prompt engineering framework that guides the model to "think aloud" by breaking complex problems down into sequential, intermediate logic steps or worked examples, significantly improving problem-solving on logic-heavy tasks.
* **Tree-of-Thought (ToT) Reasoning**
An extension of Chain-of-Thought reasoning that allows the AI model to explore, evaluate, and compare multiple reasoning branches or pathways in parallel to systematically weigh pros and cons.
* **Playoff Method (Generation and Self-Evaluation)**
Combining AI creativity with critical judgment by having the model first generate multiple diverse alternatives, critique their respective strengths against specific criteria, and finally self-select the winning solution.
* **Multimodal Prompting**
Combining visual assets (such as uploaded images or diagrams) with targeted textual instructions simultaneously to enable cross-modal reasoning and contextual analysis.
* **Few-Shot Prompting**
Providing the model with a few concrete examples of input-output pairs within the prompt to help it better interpret the intended style, format, and level of detail required.
* **Zero-Shot Prompting**
Asking the model to perform a task without providing any prior examples or demonstrations, relying entirely on its pre-trained knowledge base to execute instructions.
* **Contextual Guidance**
Supplying essential background information, framing, and situational circumstances within a prompt to align the generated response with the user's explicit goals and operating environment.
