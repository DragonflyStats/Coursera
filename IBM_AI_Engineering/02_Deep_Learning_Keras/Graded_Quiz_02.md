#### 2. Neuron Mathematics (Q4 & Q5)

* **Q4 — Resulting Weighted Sum ($z$):**


* **Given:** $x_1 = 0.9$, $w_1 = 0.1$, $x_2 = 0.4$, $w_2 = 0.6$, $b = 0.2$.


* **Formula & Calculation:**

$$z = (x_1 \cdot w_1) + (x_2 \cdot w_2) + b = (0.9 \times 0.1) + (0.4 \times 0.6) + 0.2 = 0.09 + 0.24 + 0.2 = \mathbf{0.530}$$







* **Q5 — Sigmoid Activation Output ($\sigma(z)$):**


* **Given:** $z = 1.2$, $\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$.


* **Calculation:**

$$\sigma(1.2) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-1.2}} \approx \frac{1}{1 + 0.3012} = \frac{1}{1.3012} \approx \mathbf{0.768}$$

#### 3. Biological Analogies & Network Parameters (Q6, Q7, Q10)

* **Q6 — Component Processing Electrical Impulses:**


* **Answer:** **Soma** (cell body/node where incoming signals are summed and processed).




* **Q7 — Biological Neuron Information Sequence:**


* **Answer:** **Dendrites collect incoming electrical signals and pass them to the soma, where they are processed. The processed information is then sent down the axon to the synapses, which relay it to other neurons.**




* **Q10 — Role of Weights and Biases:**


* **Answer:** **Weights determine the strength of connections between neurons, while biases provide additional flexibility in neuron activation** (weights scale input importance; biases shift the activation threshold).
