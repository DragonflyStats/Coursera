Keras is an open-source, high-level deep learning API written in Python that simplifies building, training, and deploying artificial neural networks. Created by François Chollet and guided by the philosophy of being "designed for human beings, not machines," it prioritizes developer productivity through clean, consistent, and intuitive abstractions that minimize boilerplate code.

With the release of Keras 3, the library operates as a multi-backend framework capable of running seamlessly on top of TensorFlow, JAX, and PyTorch (as well as OpenVINO for inference). This architecture allows developers to write code once and move models across different ecosystems to leverage the specific performance or deployment advantages of each engine.

Keras offers progressive disclosure of complexity through three primary model-building workflows: the **Sequential API** for straightforward stacks of layers, the **Functional API** for complex, non-linear architectures with multiple inputs or outputs, and **Model Subclassing** for full, low-level customization. Widely used across computer vision, natural language processing, and generative AI, Keras scales effortlessly from rapid prototyping on a single laptop to distributed training across large GPU and TPU clusters.
