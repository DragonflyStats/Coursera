Cognos
=======================================
IBM Cognos Business Intelligence is a web-based integrated business intelligence suite by IBM. 
It provides a toolset for reporting, analytics, scorecarding, and monitoring of events and metrics. 
The software consists of several components designed to meet the different information requirements in a company. 
IBM Cognos has components such as IBM Cognos Framework Manager, IBM Cognos Cube Designer, IBM Cognos Transformer.
