## Course Syllabus: Supervised Machine Learning & Regression

### Module 1: Introduction to Supervised Machine Learning and Linear Regression

This module introduces a brief overview of supervised machine learning and its main applications: classification and regression. After introducing the concept of regression, you will learn its best practices, as well as how to measure error and select the regression model that best suits your data.

* **Module Items:** 18 items (1–18)
* **Graded Assessment:** Module 1 Graded Quiz: Introduction to Supervised Machine Learning and Linear Regression

---

### Module 2: Data Splits and Polynomial Regression

There are a few best practices to avoid overfitting of your regression models. One of these best practices is splitting your data into training and test sets. Another alternative is to use cross-validation, and a third alternative is to introduce polynomial features. This module walks you through the theoretical framework and a few hands-on examples of these best practices.

* **Module Items:** 12 items (1–12)
* **Graded Assessment:** Module 2 Graded Quiz: Data Splits and Polynomial Regression

---

### Module 3: Cross Validation

There is a trade-off between the size of your training set and your testing set. If you use most of your data for training, you will have fewer samples to validate your model. Conversely, if you use more samples for testing, you will have fewer samples to train your model. Cross-validation allows you to reuse your data to use more samples for both training and testing.

* **Module Items:** 11 items (1–11)
* **Graded Assessment:** Module 3 Quiz: Cross Validation

---

### Module 4: Bias-Variance Trade-off and Regularization Techniques: Ridge, LASSO, and Elastic Net

This module walks you through the theory and a few hands-on examples of regularized regressions including Ridge, LASSO, and Elastic Net. You will explore the main pros and cons of these techniques, as well as their differences and similarities.

* **Module Items:** 14 items (1–14)
* **Graded Assessment:** Module 4 Graded Quiz: Bias Variance Trade off and Regularization Techniques: Ridge, LASSO, and Elastic Net

---

### Module 5: Regularization Details

In this section, you will understand the relationship between the loss function and the different regularization types.

* **Module Items:** 9 items (1–9)
* **Graded Assessment:** Module 5 Graded Quiz: Regularization Details

---

### Module 6: Final Project

In this assignment, you will apply regression techniques to analyze a dataset of your choice. Your task is to preprocess the data, build and compare models, extract insights, and suggest next steps. You will focus on presenting key findings and insights—not the code. You may include visuals to support your analysis, but the report should be a clear summary of your process and conclusions. Your final report will be evaluated by an AI-graded tool.

* **Module Items:** 4 items (1–4)
* **Graded Assessment:** AI-Graded Final Project Report
