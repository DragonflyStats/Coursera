Here are the correct answers and brief explanations for each of the four **Cognos Analytics** dashboard questions:

---

### **Question 1**

**How can you filter on a particular data field in Cognos Analytics? (Select all that apply)**

* **Correct Answers:**
* ☑ **Drag the data field to the 'This tab' area at the top of the page.**
* ☑ **Drag the data field to the 'All tabs' area at the top of the page.**


* **Explanation:** In Cognos Analytics dashboards, the top filter dock is divided into two sections: **This tab** (filters visualizations only on the active tab) and **All tabs** (applies global filters across every tab in the dashboard). You filter a field by dragging it directly from the Sources pane into either of those two filter drop zones.

---

### **Question 2**

**In Cognos Analytics what is the first thing you must do when creating a new dashboard?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Choose a template layout for your dashboard**
* **Explanation:** When you click **New > Dashboard** in Cognos Analytics, the interface immediately prompts you to select a dashboard layout template (such as a grid or tabbed layout) before adding data sources, visualizations, or widgets to the canvas.

---

### **Question 3**

**Which of the following methods can be used in Cognos Analytics to create visualizations? (Select all that apply)**

* **Correct Answers:**
* ☑ **By using the Assistant to suggest questions and visualizations.**
* ☑ **By automatic recommended visualizations.**
* ☑ **By manually populating visualizations using drag and drop.**


* **Explanation:** Cognos Analytics supports three primary workflows for generating charts: (1) typing natural-language prompts into the **AI Assistant**, (2) dragging data fields directly onto the canvas so Cognos automatically selects a **recommended visualization**, and (3) selecting a specific visualization type from the Visualizations panel and **manually dragging and dropping** fields into its slots. *(Widgets are used for layout/media elements like text, shapes, images, and web pages rather than data visualizations).*

---

### **Question 4**

**How can you create infographics in Cognos Analytics?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Drag and drop a widget shape icon onto a visualization.**
* **Explanation:** You can turn standard data fields or visualizations into custom infographics in Cognos Analytics by opening the **Widgets** pane, dragging a **shape** icon onto the canvas or visualization, and linking it to your data values (such as scaling or filling shapes dynamically based on a measure).

---
