### **Question 1**

**What are line charts best suited to show?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Trends or changes in related data over time**
* **Explanation:** Line charts connect sequential data points across continuous intervals (most commonly time), making upward/downward trends, fluctuations, and patterns over time easy to track.

---

### **Question 2**

**In a pie chart, what do all slices together represent?**

* **Correct Answer:** **100% of the whole**
* **Explanation:** A pie chart is a part-to-whole visualization in which every slice represents a proportional share of a single complete dataset, summing to 100%.

---

### **Question 3**

**Which chart type is commonly used to compare values across categories?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Bar chart**
* **Explanation:** Bar charts (and column charts) compare discrete categories side-by-side using bar lengths proportional to their values, whereas scatter and bubble charts examine relationships between numerical variables and funnel charts track sequential stage drop-offs.

---

### **Question 4**

**Which pair of software programs is commonly used to analyze data and present key findings in a data story?**

* **Correct Answer:** **PowerPoint and Excel**
* **Explanation:** **Excel** is widely used to clean, aggregate, and analyze tabular data and generate charts, while **PowerPoint** is used to structure those visuals and insights into a narrative slide deck for stakeholders.

---

### **Question 5**

**Which chart type is best for showing each car model's share of total unit sales?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Pie chart**
* **Explanation:** When showing each category's proportional share (percentage) of a single total ("share of total unit sales"), a **pie chart** directly illustrates part-to-whole composition.

---

### **Question 6**

**How does a PivotChart differ from a standard chart in Excel?**

* **Correct Answer:** **A PivotChart uses data summarized in a PivotTable**
* **Explanation:** Unlike a standard Excel chart that plots raw cell ranges directly, a PivotChart is dynamically linked to a PivotTable, automatically updating its visual layout whenever fields are grouped, filtered, expanded, or collapsed in the underlying PivotTable.

---

### **Question 7**

**What is the main difference between an area chart and a column chart?**

* **Correct Answer:** **An area chart fills the space beneath a line to emphasize magnitude over time, while a column chart uses vertical bars to compare categories**
* **Explanation:** Area charts build on line charts by shading the region below the line to highlight cumulative volume or magnitude across continuous time intervals, whereas column charts use discrete vertical bars to compare values across distinct categories.

---

**Which statements accurately describe how data visualizations support storytelling with data? *(Select all that apply)***

* **Correct Answers:**
* ☑ **Visualizations help analysts decide which data points and comparisons to emphasize.**
* ☑ **Visualizations help stakeholders see patterns, trends, and comparisons more quickly than raw data alone.**


* **Explanation:** Effective visualizations make complex datasets accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences by revealing trends and comparisons at a glance and guiding the narrative toward key takeaways.

---

### **Question 9**

**An analyst must choose a chart for a sales report. The report may either compare sales across regions or show how sales changed over the last 12 months. Which guideline best supports the chart choice?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Use a bar chart to compare sales across regions, and use a time series chart to show changes over the last 12 months.**
* **Explanation:** Categorical comparisons across discrete regions are clearest in a **bar chart**, whereas continuous temporal changes over a 12-month period are best represented with a **time series (line) chart**.

---

### **Question 10**

**You created a 3-D Clustered Column PivotChart comparing BMW, Cadillac, and Hyundai models. You want BMW models expanded in the chart while the other manufacturers remain collapsed. What should you do?**

* **Correct Answer:** **Expand BMW models in the connected PivotTable, because the PivotChart reflects the PivotTable's expansion state.**
* **Explanation:** A PivotChart mirrors the exact hierarchy and expand/collapse state of its linked PivotTable; collapsing the manufacturer field and expanding only BMW in the PivotTable immediately updates the PivotChart to show BMW's individual models alongside collapsed totals for Cadillac and Hyundai.
